Turn this photo into a clean studio pet portrait.

IDENTITY (highest priority): Keep the dog exactly as it is — same breed,
face structure, fur color and texture, eye shape and color, nose, ear
position, and markings. Do not beautify, reshape, or stylize the face.

BACKGROUND: Seamless studio backdrop, single flat neutral tone with a very
subtle top-to-bottom gradient and gentle vignette. No props, no pattern,
no floor line, no horizon.
Choose the backdrop tone based on the dog's coat so the silhouette stays
clearly separated from the background:
- White / cream / light coat  -> warm light grey  #EAE7E3
- Black / dark / grey coat    -> bright off-white #F5F3F0
- Tan / brown / mixed coat    -> neutral off-white #F0EEEB
Never let the backdrop fall within the same tonal range as the coat.
Edge separation must remain visible along the entire outline.

LIGHTING: Large softbox at 45° above-front, subtle fill from the opposite
side, soft rim light to separate fur from the background. Even and
diffused. No harsh shadows, no blown highlights. Preserve individual fur
strands, especially along the edges.

EXPRESSION: Natural and relaxed. Mouth slightly open in a normal panting
position, tongue only if already visible. Bright alert eyes with a small
natural catchlight. Do NOT create a human-like grin, do NOT widen or curl
the lips, do NOT add teeth. If the dog is not smiling in the original,
keep a calm gentle expression rather than forcing one.
If the eyes have no catchlight in the source, add one small soft catchlight
per eye at the 10–11 o'clock position. Do not enlarge or brighten the eyes.

FRAMING: Head-and-chest portrait, centered, slight negative space above
the head, eye-level camera, 85mm lens look at f/2.8 — sharp on the eyes.

GEOMETRY LOCK: Keep the original head angle and facial proportions exactly.
If the source photo is shot from above, correct only the perceived camera
height toward eye level — do NOT re-render or re-proportion the muzzle,
skull, or eye spacing to match the new angle. Preserving identity outranks
achieving a perfect eye-level look.

FINISH: Neutral white balance, natural fur tones, gentle contrast. No
filter, no HDR, no oversaturation. Clean, warm, minimal.

AVOID: forced smile, human-like grin, exaggerated teeth, cartoon eyes,
distorted muzzle, changed breed, plastic AI-smooth fur, busy background,
props, text, watermark, oversaturation, harsh shadows, wide-angle
distortion.

Output: 1:1 square, high resolution.
